Questions

What if I don't use the app?

Your manager can enter your availability for you. Every change records who made it.

What does If needed mean?

You can do the shift but would rather not. It is only used to complete the schedule, and your manager confirms it with you first.

Who can see my schedule?

Your team sees the published schedule. Fairness numbers compare you with your team's average.
